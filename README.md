**Recommendation (TL;DR):** **Hold — do not roll out the treatment.**\
Treatment conversion is slightly lower than control and the data do not support a practical or statistical improvement.

## One-line justification

The test needed about 30,300 users per group to detect the planned +0.75 pp lift and had about 145,000. The 95% CI tops out at +0.08 pp, so a lift of that size is ruled out, not merely undetected. There is no statistically significant difference (p ≈ 0.19), and the Bayesian posterior gives about a 9.5% probability that the treatment is better.

## Minimal methods summary

- **Data:** the public practice dataset from Udacity's A/B testing course: `data/ab_data.csv` (user-level A/B events) and `data/countries.csv` (user → country). The experiment was not run first-hand, so its design is reconstructed.
- **Cleaning:** 294,478 rows become 290,584 rows, one per user. 3,893 rows where the group and landing page disagree are removed, then one duplicate, keeping the **earliest** exposure per `user_id`. Mismatches are removed at row level, not user level: each of the 3,893 affected users has a second, matching row, so they stay in the analysis. Of these, 1,998 saw both pages and 1,895 saw one page under both group labels. The main results keep them, and a sensitivity check removes them. Countries are joined after cleaning, with no users lost.
- **Frequentist:** two-sided z-test for difference in proportions (p-value and 95% CI reported).
- **Bayesian:** independent Beta posterior per group with Beta(1,1) prior; posterior samples used to compute P(Treatment > Control).
- **Segmentation:** per-country Bayesian comparisons and z-tests with a Holm correction, plus a treatment × country interaction test. No country shows a significant effect, even before correction, so the decision is the same everywhere.

## Assumptions & decisions (short)

- Fixed-horizon experiment — no sequential/peeking corrections applied.
- Deduplication rule: earliest timestamp retained per user.
- The dataset does not define a conversion attribution window.
- Priors: Beta(1,1) used for clarity; no prior sensitivity analysis is in the headline results.

---

## Limitations

- Conversion-only analysis: revenue/LTV not included — perform revenue analysis to assess business impact.
- The main analysis includes 3,893 users (1.3%) with conflicting records. Removing them does not change the decision (see Validity checks).
- Country-level estimates are noisy, especially for Canada (14,499 users), and the UK's 68% probability of a lift is not evidence of one (p = 0.63, CI `[-0.00359, 0.00589]`).
- No sequential testing correction: if the experiment was monitored and stopped early, reported p-values are optimistic.

---

## Recommended next steps

1. Keep the current page.
2. If a new design is tested, track revenue per user or LTV alongside conversion, with a pre-registered MDE and fixed horizon.
